Welcome to Inkosinothando Edu Care

We believe every child deserves a safe, loving and stimulating environment. Our aim is to help children learn through play, positive interaction and age-appropriate activities.

We encourage curiosity, confidence, creativity and social development while giving children a place where they feel welcomed and valued.
